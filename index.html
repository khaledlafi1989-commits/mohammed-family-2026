Our story

My name is Mohammed Salem Mahmoud Al-Qanou’, and I am from northern Gaza. I am sharing my family’s story with hope that compassionate people will help us through the devastating circumstances we are facing.

Before the war, I lived and worked with my family and we had a stable life together. When the war began, everything changed. My family and I were forced to flee more than 15 times, moving from one place to another in search of safety. With every displacement, we lost another part of our family and our lives.

What we have lost

  • My father
  • A brother
  • A sister
  • My brother’s children
  • My grandmother
  • My grandfather
  • My aunt

We have also lost our home, our work, and almost everything we once owned. Every day that passes feels harder than the day before.

Where we are today

More than 13 members of my family are gathered together in one tent, sharing a single bathroom and living in extremely difficult conditions. Some of us are also suffering from war-related injuries and need care and support that we cannot afford.

One of my children

Has a physical disability and needs ongoing medical care and treatment.

My other child

Suffers from a chronic illness and needs ongoing medical care and treatment.

The treatment they require is not available to us here in Gaza. As a father, it is heartbreaking to see my children struggling while I am unable to provide them with the care they need.

How $50,000 will help

  • A safer temporary shelter
  • Food and clean water
  • Clothing and essential daily necessities
  • Medical care for our war-related injuries
  • Medical treatment and care for my children

We are not asking for luxury or comfort.

We are simply asking for the opportunity to protect our family, care for our children, meet our basic needs, and find a path forward after losing so much.

Every donation makes a difference

Every donation, no matter how small, can make a meaningful difference in our lives. If you are unable to donate, please consider sharing our campaign so our story can reach others who may be able to help us.
